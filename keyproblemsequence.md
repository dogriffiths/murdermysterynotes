# Key Problem-Solving Sequences

## Initial Trigger Sequence
1. "Body Found in Library" → "Miss Marple decides to investigate"
- Triggered by Colonel Bantry's wife seeking Miss Marple's help
- Critical because it brought Miss Marple's analytical skills into play
- Led to questioning two aspects simultaneously:
  * The body's identity
  * The Basil Blake connection

## Identity Investigation Sequence
1. "Identity of Body" → "Miss Marple questions fingernails"
- Key observation: Ruby's manicure habits didn't match body's fingernails
- This was the most crucial sequence in the entire case because:
  * It challenged the fundamental assumption about the victim's identity
  * Led to connecting Pamela Reeves' disappearance
  * Undermined the killers' carefully constructed alibis
  * Showed the murder was more complex than initially thought

## Basil Blake Sequence
1. "Basil Blake Connection" → "Blake decides to confess"
- Initially appeared to be main line of investigation
- Blake's confession revealed:
  * He only moved the body
  * The body was originally at his house
  * His actions inadvertently disrupted killers' plans
- Important because it:
  * Eliminated a prime suspect
  * Revealed part of the original murder plan
  * Showed why body ended up in Bantry's library

## Missing Girl Sequence
1. "Pamela Reeves Missing" → "Florence reveals film test info"
- Connected seemingly separate cases
- Florence's information revealed:
  * How Pamela was lured to hotel
  * Adelaide Jefferson's involvement
  * Method of deception used
- Crucial because it:
  * Explained how second victim was chosen
  * Revealed sophistication of murder plot
  * Provided link to Adelaide Jefferson

## Motive Sequence
1. "Motive Unknown" → "Jefferson reveals adoption plans"
- Jefferson's revelation about:
  * £50,000 inheritance
  * Plans to adopt Ruby
  * Changes to his will
- Critical because it:
  * Established clear motive
  * Showed who would benefit from Ruby's death
  * Revealed financial pressures on suspects

## Final Trap Sequence
1. "Need to Catch Killers" → "Miss Marple proposes trap"
- Used Jefferson as bait by:
  * Announcing new will plans
  * Setting up surveillance
  * Forcing killers to act
- Led to final solution through:
  * Catching killers in attempted murder
  * Confirming Miss Marple's theory
  * Providing definitive proof

## Impact Flow Analysis

Each sequence had ripple effects:
1. Identity Question → Undermined Alibis → Revealed Complex Plot
2. Blake Confession → Explained Location → Showed Original Plan
3. Florence Info → Connected Murders → Revealed Method
4. Jefferson Revelation → Established Motive → Identified Suspects
5. Trap Sequence → Caught Killers → Confirmed Theory

The success of the investigation depended on:
- Each sequence building on previous discoveries
- Multiple independent confirmations
- Key witnesses deciding to share information
- Miss Marple's ability to connect seemingly unrelated events
